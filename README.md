A one-page newspaper front page summing up world news on Friday, 20 November 2026.

## What the image shows

Masthead **THE WORLD TODAY**, dated Friday, 20 November 2026, above an original
drawn illustration of the Kyiv skyline and a bridge over the Dnipro at dawn.
The lead story covers the overnight missile-and-drone attack on Kyiv, followed
by five more stories in columns and an "Also Today" strip along the bottom.
Everything is drawn from scratch with PIL — no photographs or copied images.

## The six stories

1. **KYIV HIT AGAIN** — Fresh Russian missile-and-drone attack hits Kyiv overnight; three killed and three Dnipro bridges damaged.
2. **Markets Rout Deepens** — Nikkei down 4.8%; S&P 500 down about 2%, its fifth straight daily loss; SoftBank down over 16%; gold down 4.2%; Brent oil $90.05.
3. **Riyadh Targeted Again** — Saudi Arabia intercepts another Houthi missile and five drones, with Riyadh among the reported targets.
4. **GTA VI Tops $2.3 Billion** — Grand Theft Auto VI launch sales pass $2.3 billion, expected to be the biggest entertainment launch ever.
5. **UK Pledges 1,000+ Drones** — UK pledges more than 1,000 drones for Ukraine in a £260 million package.
6. **Trump Pardons Pete Rose** — Posthumous pardon for the late baseball star, banned for betting on games.

Also today: European youth strike in 25+ countries; UK–EU youth mobility scheme at the Brussels summit; COP31 draft drops the fossil-fuel phase-out call; Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth; El Salvador's Bukele registers for a third term.
